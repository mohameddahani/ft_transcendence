# New Member Onboarding: The First 30 Days (Glofox)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.glofox.com/blog/new-member-onboarding-beyond-the-first-workout/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   The best gym experience isn't about fancy equipment or trendy classes; it's about how members feel.
*   A great experience is defined by clarity, consistency, and how supported members feel, not just during their workouts but before, between, and after.
*   The member experience starts even before the first workout, with how easy it is to sign up and how clear communication feels.
*   Excellent programming alone isn't enough for retention; the surrounding experience, like ease of booking or asking questions, is crucial.
*   Members evaluate their gym through many small interactions, not just one big decision.
*   Consistency in messaging, scheduling, payments, and communication builds trust and emotional comfort, which keeps members coming back.
*   Technology can help deliver a consistent experience by simplifying logistics and freeing staff to focus on member connections.
*   Most gyms lose members due to small, unnoticed "experience gaps" like clunky booking or inconsistent communication, rather than major mistakes.

## What a gym can do

*   Focus on making every interaction easy, supportive, and consistent for members.
*   Ensure the sign-up process is straightforward and communication is clear from the very beginning.
*   Create a seamless experience across all touchpoints, from booking to staff interactions, so members can focus on their workouts without logistical worries.
*   Remove any friction points that might make the experience difficult to navigate, such as unclear booking procedures or hesitation to ask questions.
*   Prioritize consistency in all aspects of the gym's operations, including messaging, scheduling, and payments, to build member confidence and trust.
*   Utilize technology to streamline bookings, centralize communication, and reduce administrative tasks, allowing staff to dedicate more time to personal connections with members.
*   Actively identify and address small experience gaps, such as silence between visits or overly manual processes, before they erode member trust and lead to disengagement.
*   Design an overall experience that feels supportive beyond just the workout itself, fostering long-term commitment from members.
