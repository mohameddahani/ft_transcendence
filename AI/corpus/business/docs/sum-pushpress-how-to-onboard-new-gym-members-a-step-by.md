# How to Onboard New Gym Members: A Step-by-Step Guide (PushPress)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/onboarding-new-gym-members). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   **Onboarding is crucial for retention:** Many gym owners focus on attracting new members but lack a plan for their first week, which is where retention is often won or lost. A good onboarding experience sets the tone for a member's entire journey.
*   **Poor onboarding leads to churn:** A survey found that a majority of gyms send new clients directly to group classes or rely on free trials, both of which are associated with higher member turnover.
*   **Structured onboarding improves retention:** Even a small number of one-on-one sessions, as few as five, can significantly improve member retention.
*   **Three common onboarding methods exist:**
    *   **Personal Training (PT) First:** Best for beginners or intimidated members, and gyms selling PT. It builds strong coach relationships and can increase revenue, but might create trainer-dependent members and be hard to scale.
    *   **Group Fundamentals:** A middle-ground option suitable for most boutique gyms. It offers a personal touch at a lower price and eases members into group settings, but cohort start dates can create bottlenecks. This typically involves a course lasting four to six weeks.
    *   **Direct-to-Class:** Best for experienced members transferring from other gyms. This is the weakest option for true beginners, who tend to churn at higher rates.
*   **Tailor onboarding to experience:** The less experienced a new member is, the more guided their onboarding should be. Sending a nervous beginner straight into a busy class is a quick way to lose them.
*   **Essential onboarding elements:** Regardless of the method, a strong process should personalize the experience, establish a baseline (goals, injuries, measurements), provide a relevant tour, teach safe movement, set clear expectations, introduce them to the community, invite them to events, answer all questions patiently, and automate follow-ups.

## What a gym can do

*   **Choose an appropriate onboarding method:** Select a method (PT first, group fundamentals, or direct-to-class) that aligns with your gym's model, pricing, and the typical experience level of your new members.
*   **Implement a personalized approach:** Talk to each new member to understand their individual goals, fears, and story, then build a plan tailored to them.
*   **Establish a starting baseline:** Record their fitness experience, any injuries, and if they are willing, take measurements or conduct a simple benchmark test. This provides a starting point to track and celebrate progress.
*   **Provide a meaningful tour:** Instead of just showing equipment, walk them through what a first class is like, where briefings happen, how movements can be modified, and where successes are celebrated.
*   **Prioritize safe movement instruction:** Use early one-on-one time to coach proper technique and assess mobility limitations at the member's own pace to prevent injuries.
*   **Set clear expectations:** Give new members an overview of their journey, including how to book classes, milestones to aim for, and a realistic timeline for results to prevent early disappointment.
*   **Integrate them into the community:** Introduce every new member to at least three people: an enthusiastic existing member, someone who trains at their usual time, and a coach who is good with beginners.
*   **Invite them to community events:** Encourage new members to attend social events outside of regular classes, and suggest they bring a friend to ease social pressure and potentially attract new members.
*   **Be patient and answer all questions:** Recognize that new members experience information overload and patiently answer their questions, even if they seem repetitive. Remind them that consistency is key and everyone has off days.
*   **Automate follow-up communications:** Use software to automate welcome texts on day one, app setup emails, birthday messages, milestone celebrations, and progress check-ins to maintain consistent communication.
*   **Utilize a simple onboarding checklist:** Create a short, repeatable checklist for your front desk staff to ensure every new sign-up receives consistent onboarding. This should include capturing goals, booking initial sessions, making introductions, sending welcome messages, completing onboarding sessions, celebrating early wins, and inviting them to events.
*   **Leverage software for consistency:** Use gym management software to automate welcome and follow-up communications, streamline scheduling for PT or group fundamentals, and maintain detailed member notes and history so any coach can continue the relationship seamlessly.
