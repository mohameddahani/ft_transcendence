# New Member Onboarding Guide (ABC Fitness)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://abcfitness.com/abc-articles/new-member-onboarding-guide/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   The first four weeks of a new membership are vital for breaking down barriers and engaging members.
*   It's more cost-effective to retain existing members than to acquire new ones.
*   A structured 90-day onboarding journey, using texts, emails, and calls, is crucial for new members.
*   Educated members are more engaged members.
*   Rewarding members for accomplishments, big or small, helps reinforce positive behaviors.
*   Proactively seeking feedback from new members helps identify pain points and shows you value their opinions.
*   Introducing staff members helps new members feel comfortable and build connections.
*   Celebrating milestones, like a first month or a certain number of workouts, reinforces member efforts.
*   Fostering community among members through events or introductions strengthens relationships and accountability.
*   Continuously communicating about goals, challenges, and promotions keeps members engaged.
*   Regularly asking for feedback and demonstrating how you've made improvements shows you care.
*   Building personal relationships with members beyond a simple "hello" creates emotional connections and shows you value them.

## What a gym can do

*   **Outline a 90-day onboarding journey:** Plan a series of communications (text, email, phone calls) for new members, then add them to your regular newsletter.
*   **Send a welcome email:** Include information on club areas, parking, locker rooms, and a call-to-action to book ancillary services like personal training.
*   **Offer a complimentary personal training session:** Provide a free one-hour session to all new members.
*   **Reward accomplishments:** Send messages after a few workouts or completed personal training sessions, offering a discount or asking about their next booking to encourage continued engagement.
*   **Ask for feedback:** Send a survey to new members, making them feel valued, providing a clear link, and setting expectations for completion time. Consider offering an incentive for completing the survey.
*   **Introduce staff:** Send an email with brief, fun bios of your employees to help members put faces to names and build rapport.
*   **Celebrate milestones:** Set up automated messages to congratulate members on their first month, a certain number of classes, or check-ins, reinforcing positive behavior.
*   **Build community:** Organize events like happy hours or holiday parties, or facilitate introductions between members.
*   **Communicate goals and promotions:** Use emails and texts to inform new members about challenges, personal training offers, and class schedules.
*   **Regularly ask for feedback:** Follow up with members after two to three months to ask for their thoughts again, and share any improvements made based on feedback.
*   **Build personal connections:** Encourage staff to learn about members' lives, hobbies, and interests to create deeper relationships.
