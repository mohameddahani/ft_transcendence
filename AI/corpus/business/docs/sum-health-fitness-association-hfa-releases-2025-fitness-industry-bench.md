# HFA Releases 2025 Fitness Industry Benchmarking Report (Health & Fitness Association)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.healthandfitness.org/hfa-releases-2025-fitness-industry-benchmarking-report/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   The Health & Fitness Association (HFA) has released its 2025 Fitness Industry Benchmarking Report, which is the most thorough analysis of financial and operational performance in the fitness sector since 2019.
*   This report is based on data collected between April and June 2025 from 175 companies, representing over 17,000 fitness facilities across 27 countries.
*   The findings indicate that health and fitness facilities are maintaining strong profitability and offering significant financial opportunities.
*   Median revenue saw an increase of 9.9% compared to 2023.
*   EBITDA margins reached a median of 23.6%, with a majority of clubs reporting positive margins.
*   Net membership grew by an average of 5.5%.
*   Member retention for the year averaged 66.4%.
*   The report includes various benchmarks such as income generated per club, the mix of membership dues and other income, levels of reinvestment, different profitability ratios, how well members are kept, the types of programs offered, and available amenities and services.
*   It also provides detailed breakdowns by club size, type, and membership fee structure, offering insights into the performance of different business models.
*   HFA's President & CEO, Liz Clark, stated that the report confirms the fitness industry is a profitable business, highlighting both its financial success and the investments operators are making to ensure sustained growth.

## What a gym can do

*   Gyms can use this report to measure and improve their own performance by comparing their results against industry peers.
*   The benchmarks cover critical areas like income and profitability, financial health, membership trends, how efficiently facilities are used, and staffing and payroll details.
*   By benchmarking, operators can identify inefficiencies before they impact profitability, assess staff productivity to adjust staffing models, and set realistic growth targets based on data.
*   The report can help strengthen business plans and investor pitches by providing credible industry benchmarks.
*   Clubs can learn from top performers in the industry and adopt their successful strategies.
*   Operators who participated in the survey received a confidential report comparing their performance to industry norms, a report card highlighting areas for improvement, and access to interactive online tools for deeper analysis.
*   HFA also recently launched a 2025 Employee Compensation & Benefits Survey, which focuses on staffing, pay, and benefits, providing a valuable complement to financial benchmarking for navigating the current labor market.
*   Club owners and operators in the U.S. and Canada are encouraged to participate in the Employee Compensation & Benefits Survey to help build reliable workforce benchmarks and gain insights for their own staffing strategies.
