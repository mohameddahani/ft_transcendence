# 7 Gym Email Templates for Fitness Studios (Glofox)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.glofox.com/blog/gym-email-templates/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   Email remains a highly effective way to engage with your gym members and maintain high retention rates, despite the rise of other communication methods.
*   Email open rates have significantly increased since 2011, more than doubling, with a majority of emails, sixty percent, now opened on mobile devices, often due to push notifications.
*   Consistent, valuable engagement is crucial for retaining members longer, which directly leads to increased recurring revenue for your gym.
*   An effective retention email strategy involves sending the right message at the right time through the appropriate channel.
*   There are four main types of retention emails: those celebrating milestones and accomplishments (like welcome or anniversary emails), those promoting events and challenges, educational content (such as recipes or workout videos), and prompts and reminders (especially for class bookings or inactive members).
*   Dr. Paul Bedford, a fitness retention expert, emphasizes that the primary goal of every email should be to encourage a member visit or a membership renewal.
*   Key elements of a stellar retention email include personalization (even just using a member's name), engaging and memorable content with a strong subject line, a design that encourages quick scanning and action, language that resonates with the customer, and simple, clear messaging.

## What a gym can do

*   Implement a structured email strategy to engage members, as email is a powerful tool for nurturing leads and keeping members connected to your brand.
*   Map out the entire member journey from the moment they sign up, ensuring a clear and consistent messaging plan for each stage.
*   Utilize modern tools and automation to send personalized emails and push notifications, saving significant time compared to manual methods and ensuring a consistent experience for all members.
*   Develop and use various email templates for different purposes, such as welcome emails for new members, referral program promotions, post-workout congratulations, class reminders, re-engagement emails for inactive members, follow-up emails for abandoned bookings, and special offers or promotions.
*   Personalize emails as much as possible, for example, by including the member's name or sending automated birthday offers.
*   Craft engaging and memorable emails with compelling subject lines, focusing on a personal touch and getting straight to the point.
*   Design emails to be easily scannable and visually appealing, ensuring they offer immediate value and encourage action from the reader.
*   Use language that your members understand and can relate to, breaking down complex fitness information into motivating and actionable terms.
*   Keep email messages simple and clear, avoiding overly long or complex content that might deter readers.
*   Consider offering incentives for referrals to turn existing members into advocates for your gym.
*   Automate class reminder emails to help reduce cancellations and boost attendance, providing a gentle nudge for members to stick to their workout commitments.
*   Proactively reach out to inactive members with offers or valuable content to re-engage them and reduce attrition.
*   Send follow-up emails for specific behaviors, such as abandoned class bookings or merchandise purchases, to prompt members to complete their actions.
*   Use email marketing to promote new classes, special offers, and upsell digital memberships or other products to both existing and new customers.
