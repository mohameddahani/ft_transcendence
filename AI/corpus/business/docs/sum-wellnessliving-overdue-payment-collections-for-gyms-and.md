# Overdue Payment Collections for Gyms and Fitness Studios (WellnessLiving)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.wellnessliving.com/blog/overdue-payment-collections-gyms-fitness-studios/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   A significant number of gym memberships, specifically 63%, are not used at all, and half of new members stop attending after three months.
*   When members stop coming, they often neglect to update their payment details, leading to missed monthly payments.
*   Unpaid membership fees directly impact a gym's financial health and its ability to expand.
*   A single missed payment might seem minor, but many missed payments can create substantial financial problems for gyms.
*   The average gym membership costs around $50 per month, with boutique studios charging $90 or more, meaning significant revenue is at risk. For example, 50 non-paying members could result in a loss of tens of thousands of dollars annually.
*   Many general collection agencies are not suitable for the fitness industry, as they focus on larger, more traditional debts.
*   Nearly half of gym membership cancellations, 46%, are due to cost, which can also lead to members ending up in collections.
*   Gym staff are typically not trained as debt collectors and should focus on member engagement and new sign-ups rather than chasing overdue payments.
*   It's important to use respectful and understanding communication when dealing with overdue payments, as many clients face temporary financial difficulties.

## What a gym can do

*   Regularly review and update client payment information to prevent issues.
*   Identify accounts with past due payments promptly.
*   Prioritize preserving the client relationship even when collecting overdue payments.
*   Consider partnering with a collection service that specializes in the fitness industry.
*   Utilize business management software to keep client personal and financial information organized and up-to-date in one place.
*   With such software, you can adjust payment schedules, put accounts on hold, track attendance and payment history, and monitor unpaid fees.
*   The software can also help you review payment methods and proactively communicate with clients about expiring credit cards.
*   Implement automated billing systems that immediately retry failed payments, as many issues stem from expired cards or outdated details.
*   Send proactive reminders via email, SMS, or app notifications to resolve payment issues before accounts become overdue.
*   Start with friendly reminders and gradually increase urgency with follow-ups and clear payment deadlines.
*   Track and view reports showing active or inactive members with negative balances, clients who are 90 days past due, and their collection status.
*   The ultimate goals of payment collections are to receive outstanding balances and preserve the client relationship, potentially allowing for membership reinstatement.
*   After a client has paid their debt, re-engage with them by offering a discounted membership renewal to regain their trust.
*   Use AI-powered reports to identify clients at risk of leaving in the near future, with a high accuracy rate, and then set up automated campaigns to reach out to them.
*   Consider outsourcing collections to specialized agencies that work exclusively with gyms to save time and improve recovery rates, while protecting your brand's reputation.
*   Establish a clear process for collections, typically considering escalation after about 90 days of non-payment, ensuring all communication steps are documented.
*   Offer flexible payment options to resolve issues faster and maintain positive long-term relationships.
