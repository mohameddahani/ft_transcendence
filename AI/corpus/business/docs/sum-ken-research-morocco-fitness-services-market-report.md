# Morocco Fitness Services Market Report (Ken Research)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/morocco-fitness-services-market). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   The fitness market in Morocco is growing significantly, projected to nearly double in value from a hundred and eighty-five million US dollars in two thousand and twenty-five to three hundred and fifty million US dollars by two thousand and thirty-one.
*   This growth is driven by an increasing urban population, with over sixty percent of Moroccans living in cities, which makes it easier to set up and manage multiple gym locations.
*   Membership numbers are expected to rise substantially, from around seven hundred and twelve thousand active paid members in two thousand and twenty-five to approximately one point one five million by two thousand and thirty-one.
*   While full-service clubs are currently the main revenue source, there's a strong trend towards "hybrid memberships" that combine physical access with digital programs and remote coaching.
*   Profitability is shifting from basic gym access to additional services like personal training, small-group classes, wellness programs, nutrition advice, and digital engagement.
*   Morocco has a high prevalence of non-communicable diseases and insufficient physical activity, creating a strong demand for health and prevention-focused fitness services.
*   The market is fragmented, with many independent clubs, but national chains are expanding and benefiting from shared resources like procurement and marketing.

## What a gym can do

*   **Focus on Hybrid Memberships:** Offer flexible membership options that combine in-club access with mobile app programming, remote coaching, and multi-club access to meet evolving customer expectations and improve retention.
*   **Diversify Revenue Streams:** Move beyond basic access fees by offering a wider range of services such as personal training, small-group classes, recovery services, nutrition support, and premium wellness add-ons. This can increase the average revenue per member.
*   **Leverage Technology:** Implement integrated apps and shared member databases to track engagement, identify opportunities for upgrades, and manage member retention more effectively.
*   **Consider Scalable Formats:** Explore compact club designs or multi-site models that balance market access with capital control, especially in secondary cities where there's less competition.
*   **Target Urban Areas:** Focus expansion efforts in major cities and urban corridors where the population density supports higher utilization and multi-site networks.
*   **Address Health Needs:** Develop programs and services that cater to the high demand for prevention-oriented fitness, weight management, and general physical activity, potentially partnering with employers for corporate wellness initiatives.
*   **Maintain Capital Discipline:** Be mindful of fixed costs associated with imported equipment, regulatory compliance, and large club footprints. Prioritize networks with strong site-level economics and diversified membership products.
