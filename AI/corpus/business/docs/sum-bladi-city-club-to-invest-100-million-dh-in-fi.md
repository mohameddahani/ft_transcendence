# City Club to Invest 100 Million DH in Fitness Equipment, Plans 40 New Women-Only Gyms in Morocco (Bladi)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://en.bladi.net/city-club-invest-100-million-fitness-equipment-plans-women-only-gyms-morocco,60825.html). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   City Club, now known as 24 Hours City Club, has partnered with an American investment fund called Spartan Investments.
*   Spartan Investments has acquired a significant portion of City Club's capital, specifically 35%, for an amount between 200 and 300 million DH.
*   This partnership aims to support City Club's growth, both through its own expansion and by identifying and executing external growth opportunities.
*   24 Hours City Club plans to invest a substantial amount, 100 million DH, specifically in new fitness equipment.
*   They have already placed orders for new machines from Matrix, a leading global supplier, which are expected to be delivered soon.
*   A major part of their expansion strategy includes opening 40 new gym locations that will be exclusively for women.

## What a gym can do

*   Consider strategic partnerships with investment funds to secure capital for significant expansion and equipment upgrades.
*   Explore opportunities to specialize or cater to specific demographics, such as opening women-only facilities, to tap into new market segments.
*   Invest in high-quality, new fitness equipment from reputable global leaders to enhance member experience and attract new clients.
*   Develop a clear growth strategy that includes both expanding existing operations and potentially acquiring other businesses.
*   Leverage external investment to support ambitious expansion plans, such as opening multiple new locations.
