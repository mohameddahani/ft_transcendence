# How to Create and Implement a Fitness Referral Program That Works (Mindbody)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.mindbodyonline.com/business/education/blog/fitness-referral-program). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   Word-of-mouth is a powerful way to grow your gym, and a referral program turns this natural enthusiasm into a structured way to get new clients.
*   Referral programs help your gym grow in the short term and keep members longer by turning happy clients into active promoters.
*   They build loyalty by giving members a reason to stay involved beyond their workouts, making them feel more connected to your brand.
*   Referral programs are a cost-effective way to get new clients compared to paid advertising and promotions, as they use your most trusted channel: existing clients.
*   Once set up, these programs can largely run on their own, providing a steady stream of new leads without constant ad spending.
*   Clients who come through referrals tend to sign up faster, stay longer, and participate more because they already trust your gym and understand what you offer.
*   There are different types of referral programs:
    *   **Member-to-member:** Rewards existing members for referring new clients.
    *   **Dual-sided:** Both the referrer and the new client get a reward, which often increases participation.
    *   **Single-sided:** Only the referring member gets a reward, suitable if your members are already very engaged.
    *   **Time-based/promotional:** Run for a limited time to create urgency, good for slow periods or special promotions.
*   Incentives are crucial and should be valuable to members while being affordable for your gym. Options include:
    *   **Monetary:** Account credits, discounted memberships, or money off future purchases.
    *   **Retail products:** Branded merchandise, supplements, or apparel, which also act as advertising.
    *   **Service-based:** Free personal training sessions, class packs, or temporary access to premium services, which can also lead to upsells.

## What a gym can do

*   **Set clear goals:** Decide what you want to achieve, such as increasing trial visits or new memberships, to guide your program structure.
*   **Offer dual-sided rewards:** Reward both the existing member and the new client to boost participation and conversion.
*   **Keep rules simple:** Clearly define who can be referred, how referrals are tracked, and when rewards are given to avoid confusion.
*   **Automate tracking:** Use your gym management software to automate referral codes or links to reduce manual work and ensure accurate reward delivery.
*   **Train staff:** Equip your team with information and talking points so they can promote the program naturally to members.
*   **Promote consistently:** Highlight the program through emails, texts, social media, and in-gym signs, using periodic reminders or limited-time bonuses to keep interest high.
*   **Monitor and refine:** Track key metrics like participation and conversion rates to adjust incentives, messages, and timing for better results.
