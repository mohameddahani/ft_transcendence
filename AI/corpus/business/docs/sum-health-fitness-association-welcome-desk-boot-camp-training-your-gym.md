# Welcome Desk Boot Camp: Training Your Gym's Front-line Staff (Health & Fitness Association)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.healthandfitness.org/improve-your-club/welcome-desk-boot-camp-training-your-gyms-front-line-staff/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   The Health & Fitness Association is a non-profit global trade association focused on supporting and growing the health and fitness industry.
*   It has been operating for over forty years, partnering with gym owners, operators, and industry leaders.
*   The association emphasizes the importance of its staff's expertise and passion for health and fitness in achieving its mission.
*   The organization's team is dedicated to helping its members succeed, which in turn helps the industry thrive.

## What a gym can do

*   Gyms can benefit from the association's support in building strong, thriving businesses.
*   Gym owners and operators can view the association as a partner that understands the requirements for success in the industry.
*   Gyms can leverage the association's dedication to their success to help their own businesses flourish.
