# Gym Debt Collection: Recover Unpaid Membership Fees (ABC Fitness)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://abcfitness.com/abc-articles/gym-debt-collection/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   **Payment issues are a major problem:** Almost twenty-one percent of gym cancellations are due to problems with collecting payments, which is a significant source of members leaving that could be prevented.
*   **Modern members expect digital solutions:** A large majority of new gym members are from younger generations who are used to managing tasks digitally. They expect to resolve payment issues quickly and easily online, without needing to make calls or fill out paperwork.
*   **Debt collection is part of the member relationship:** How you handle unpaid fees impacts member trust and your brand. A respectful and clear process can help recover money and keep members connected to your gym.
*   **Common reasons for unpaid fees:** Members often fall behind because their payment information is out of date, they are experiencing financial difficulties, they are confused about their cancellation, or they can't get answers to their billing questions.
*   **Financial impact of poor practices:** Most gyms lose between five and nine percent of their expected income due to payment failures. Additionally, staff time spent chasing overdue accounts takes away from helping members and making sales, and inconsistent follow-up creates more administrative work. Poor communication can also lead to negative reviews and members not returning.

## What a gym can do

*   **Implement a staged recovery process:** Start by preventing issues and resolving missed payments early with automated retries and polite communication. Then, offer reasonable solutions when appropriate, and only escalate after following documented steps and legal review.
*   **Automate payment retries and communication:** Use automated systems to retry failed payments and send scheduled reminders. This handles routine tasks, freeing up staff for more complex issues.
*   **Offer self-service options:** Provide members with easy ways to update their payment details, ask questions, or manage their accounts through a branded app.
*   **Have a clear escalation process:** If initial measures don't resolve an overdue balance, follow a consistent, documented process for escalation, including legal review.
*   **Understand and address common payment failure reasons:** Use automated card updater technology for expired cards. For members facing financial hardship, consider offering payment plans or temporary freezes. Prevent cancellation confusion by providing clear billing and cancellation information at signup.
*   **Maintain clear records:** Document all payment attempts, notices, cancellations, and communications. This helps manage delinquent accounts and provides a clear record if a member disputes a balance.
*   **Consider outside collection agencies carefully:** If referring an account to an agency, vet them thoroughly, confirming their licensing, communication standards, and experience with gym debt. Be aware that sending an account to collections doesn't automatically affect a member's credit report; that requires separate reporting to a credit bureau.
*   **Adopt ethical best practices:** Approach conversations about unpaid fees without accusation, protect member privacy by discussing issues in private channels, and offer practical solutions like payment plans or temporary freezes.
*   **Focus on prevention strategies:** Clearly explain billing, cancellation rules, and consequences of failed payments in membership agreements. Collect a backup payment method at signup, send expiration alerts for cards, and make it easy for members to freeze, cancel, or update payment details. Use engagement data to identify disengaged members early.
