# Best Fitness Onboarding Strategies for New Members (Virtuagym)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://business.virtuagym.com/blog/fitness-onboarding/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   **Onboarding is crucial for retention:** Getting new clients to sign up is only the first step; the real challenge is keeping them. A weak onboarding process means new members will likely leave quickly.
*   **New members often feel lost:** Many people don't know where to start in a gym and can feel awkward or intimidated. A clear onboarding process removes this confusion and provides a starting point.
*   **Comfort and belonging are vital:** New members need to feel comfortable and like they belong from day one. This helps them overcome feelings of not fitting in and encourages them to stay.
*   **Habit formation is key:** People stick with fitness when it becomes a habit, not just because of initial motivation. The first few weeks are critical for establishing consistency and building momentum.
*   **Retention impacts your business:** Clients who go through a structured onboarding process are much more likely to stay long-term, leading to fewer cancellations and increased member engagement.
*   **The first thirty days are make-or-break:** New members often decide within their first month whether they will continue with a gym. Your efforts during this period are essential for long-term retention.
*   **Digital onboarding is a must:** In the current environment, a digital component to your onboarding process is non-negotiable, offering convenience and accessibility.
*   **Common mistakes lead to drop-offs:** Issues like unclear next steps, information overload, generic experiences, lack of follow-up, no emotional connection, ignoring warning signs, and relying only on in-person onboarding can cause members to quit.

## What a gym can do

*   **Create a personalized welcome:** Greet new members by name, offer a studio tour, introduce them to staff and other members, and send a personal follow-up message after their first session.
*   **Implement a structured kickoff session:** This session should include a goal-setting conversation to understand their "why," a movement assessment to identify limitations, a challenging but achievable first workout, and a clear roadmap for their next steps.
*   **Engage members during the first month:** Schedule follow-ups after their first, third, and fifth sessions. Consider assigning an accountability partner, celebrate their early wins, and actively ask for feedback to show you care and improve your process.
*   **Integrate digital tools:** Use a welcome email sequence, a mobile app or online portal for booking and tracking, and on-demand video content for tutorials and etiquette.
*   **Develop a "Trial Success Plan":** For trial memberships, create a step-by-step roadmap for their first month, guiding them through initial sessions, goal setting, and progress reviews.
*   **Pre-frame renewals early:** Position membership as a long-term lifestyle choice. Offer renewal incentives like a free personal training session or exclusive class access, and consistently show members their progress to reinforce their commitment.
*   **Utilize fitness studio management software:** This streamlines sign-ups, scheduling, progress tracking, and automated reminders, acting as a central hub for managing memberships and communication.
*   **Offer a dedicated mobile app:** Provide a self-guided onboarding experience, habit tracking, virtual coaching, and community features like leaderboards and group chats.
*   **Implement automated CRM:** Use a CRM system to automate personalized follow-ups, send onboarding email sequences, and provide membership renewal reminders.
*   **Consider AI-powered chatbots:** Offer instant answers to frequently asked questions, personalized recommendations, and 24/7 support to new members.
*   **Provide video-based onboarding:** Use pre-recorded welcome videos, virtual tours, and workout tutorials to ease anxiety and prepare new members before they even arrive.
*   **Integrate wearable fitness technology and gamification:** Encourage motivation by allowing members to track progress, participate in challenges, and engage in friendly competition.
