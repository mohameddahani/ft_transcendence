# Client Profile: City Club (Virtuagym)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://business.virtuagym.com/customer/city-club/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   City Club, a gym chain in Morocco, North Africa, successfully expanded from over 20 locations to more than 50 locations, achieving a goal they set in 2018.
*   They offer a variety of facilities and services, catering to members with different budget sizes.
*   City Club partnered with Virtuagym to support their growth and enhance member experiences across their numerous locations.
*   Their approach includes educating members on nutrition to help them achieve their fitness goals.
*   They foster engagement and improve member retention through an online community that features tailored interest groups.
*   Visualizing member progress is a key strategy to maintain motivation and commitment.
*   City Club's expansion demonstrates their strong presence in the Moroccan fitness market.

## What a gym can do

*   Set ambitious growth targets, as City Club did by aiming to more than double their locations.
*   Offer diverse facilities and services to attract a wide range of members, accommodating various budget levels.
*   Consider implementing a software solution that can scale with rapid business growth, similar to City Club's choice.
*   Prioritize educating members on nutrition to help them achieve their personal fitness objectives.
*   Build an online community with specific interest groups to drive member engagement and improve retention rates.
*   Utilize tools or methods to visualize member progress, which can help keep members motivated and committed to their fitness journey.
*   Focus on delivering exceptional fitness experiences to set new industry standards.
