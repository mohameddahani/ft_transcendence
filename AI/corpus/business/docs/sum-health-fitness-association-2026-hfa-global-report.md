# 2026 HFA Global Report (Health & Fitness Association)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.healthandfitness.org/2026-hfa-global-report/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   The 2026 Global Report from the Health & Fitness Association (HFA) offers a comprehensive, data-driven overview of the commercial health and fitness industry.
*   It covers 33 countries and five major regions: Asia-Pacific, Europe, Latin America, the Middle East, and North America.
*   The report combines industry data with insights from national federations and market experts to analyze the forces shaping global fitness markets.
*   It includes information on market structure and segmentation, over 200 operator profiles, and global rankings of top fitness companies based on 2025 revenue, facility count, and membership.
*   New additions to this year's report include a "Global Operator Snapshot" detailing 2025 performance metrics like revenue, membership, retention, and profitability.
*   It also features "2026 Expectations," providing insights into operator sentiment regarding future revenue, membership, EBITDA, staffing, and technology investment.
*   The report draws on various sources, including HFA's global operator survey, proprietary HFA research, data from national fitness federations, independent audits, company disclosures, and historical data.
*   The HFA also offers a "FIT Tracker" which monitors fitness facility traffic, noting strong growth in August 2026, particularly in high-value, low-price (HVLP) facilities and studios.
*   Another resource, a white paper, discusses the impact of GLP-1 medications on obesity care and the new opportunities they present for structured exercise.

## What a gym can do

*   You can use the HFA's 2026 Global Report to understand the size, performance, and direction of the health and fitness industry worldwide.
*   The report can help you benchmark your gym's performance against global trends by looking at operator performance data for 2025, including revenue, membership, retention, and profitability.
*   You can gain insights into future industry trends and operator sentiment for 2026 regarding revenue, membership, staffing, and technology investments, which can inform your strategic planning.
*   The report's country profiles, where data is available, can help you understand market structure and segmentation relevant to your region.
*   By reviewing the global rankings of leading fitness companies, you can see how larger players are performing in terms of revenue, facility count, and membership.
*   You can also explore the HFA's "FIT Tracker" to stay updated on fitness facility traffic trends, which might help you understand broader market activity.
*   Consider the implications of GLP-1 medications, as discussed in the HFA's white paper, for your gym's offerings, particularly in relation to weight loss and structured exercise programs.
