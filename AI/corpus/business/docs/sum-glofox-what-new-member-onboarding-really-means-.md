# What New Member Onboarding Really Means for Gyms and Studios (Glofox)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.glofox.com/blog/new-member-onboarding/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   New member onboarding is more than just a welcome email and a gym tour; it's a guided experience that helps new members feel confident, connected, and consistent during their first month.
*   Many cancellations happen because members feel unsure, overwhelmed, or disconnected early on, not because they dislike the workouts.
*   Effective onboarding builds confidence by reassuring members they belong and are supported, setting expectations, reducing friction, and turning initial enthusiasm into regular attendance.
*   Onboarding is a journey that starts the moment someone joins, not when they first walk through the door, and continues over their first few weeks.
*   Key steps in effective onboarding include clear communication at every stage, simple education to support success, and accessible human support for questions.
*   Common mistakes include overwhelming new members with too much information, assuming they will figure things out on their own, or treating onboarding as complete after the first visit.
*   Onboarding typically lasts for the first thirty days, though small follow-ups beyond this period can also help with retention.

## What a gym can do

*   Ensure the first communication after someone joins clearly welcomes them, explains what happens next, and removes uncertainty about booking classes, what to bring, and what their first visit will be like.
*   Make the first in-person or virtual experience welcoming with a friendly greeting, quick orientation, or simple check-in to make members feel comfortable and seen.
*   Prioritize ease for new members by making booking simple, schedules clear, and expectations well-explained to help them feel capable rather than overwhelmed during their initial visits.
*   Focus on delivering the right information at the right time through clear instructions, timely reminders, and consistent messaging, avoiding both too much and too little information.
*   Provide brief explanations of class formats, equipment, etiquette, or how to use the gym's app to empower members and reduce stress.
*   Ensure support is accessible and human, whether through front-desk staff, coaches, or automated check-ins, so questions can be answered quickly.
*   Utilize technology, such as studio management platforms, to automate scheduling, payments, member communications, and app access, ensuring a consistent onboarding experience as the gym grows.
*   By using systems for logistics, staff can focus on building connections with new members, reducing barriers during their first month.
