# The rise of the fitness industry in Morocco: trends and opportunities (UOC Management & eLearning blog)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://blogs.uoc.edu/mel/the-rise-of-the-fitness-industry-in-morocco-trends-and-opportunities/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   The fitness industry in Morocco is growing significantly, driven by several factors.
*   Morocco has become more politically and economically stable, and structural reforms have improved the business environment.
*   The population is young but also experiencing increased life expectancy, suggesting an aging demographic in the future.
*   People have busier lifestyles and more money to spend, which is changing the fitness and wellness sector.
*   A high percentage of Moroccans suffer from chronic diseases like diabetes, high cholesterol, and high blood pressure, leading to greater health awareness and increased physical activity.
*   Women are playing a key role in this shift, as more of them are working and focusing on their physical appearance.
*   There are different types of fitness centers: premium chains offering luxury services at high prices, low-cost chains making sport accessible with lower prices, and specialized boutique centers providing personalized services in smaller spaces.
*   Marketing strategies vary: premium centers rely on exclusivity and customer recommendations, low-cost centers focus on price and mass marketing, and boutique centers use micro-marketing for specific audiences.
*   Leading centers retain customers by offering comprehensive services beyond just exercise, such as healthy restaurants, beauty salons, and high-end hairdressers.
*   Some centers also use mobile apps to enhance customer relationships and provide extra benefits.

## What a gym can do

*   Consider the demographic shifts in your area, including an aging population and increased purchasing power, to tailor your offerings.
*   Highlight the health benefits of exercise, especially in relation to chronic diseases, to appeal to a health-conscious clientele.
*   Recognize the growing influence of women in the fitness market and consider how to attract and retain them.
*   Decide on your business model: will you be a premium, low-cost, or boutique center, or a hybrid? Each has distinct target audiences and pricing strategies.
*   Align your marketing efforts with your chosen business model: focus on exclusivity and referrals for premium, price for low-cost, or highly segmented micro-marketing for boutique.
*   Think about offering services beyond just fitness classes or equipment, such as healthy food options, beauty services, or other wellness amenities, to build customer loyalty.
*   Explore using mobile apps to engage with members, offer additional benefits, and strengthen customer relationships.
