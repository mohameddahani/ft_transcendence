# Gym Onboarding Process to Boost Member Retention (GymMaster)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.gymmaster.com/blog/gym-onboarding-process-to-boost-member-retention/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   **High Member Churn:** Half of all new gym members cancel within the first six months, which is a significant loss of the time, money, and effort spent on attracting them.
*   **Onboarding Boosts Retention:** A positive and high-quality onboarding process can increase member retention to 87%.
*   **Retention Saves and Makes Money:** It is significantly cheaper to keep an existing customer than to acquire a new one, and loyal customers spend more. Even a small increase in retention can lead to a substantial boost in profits.
*   **Critical First 90 Days:** The initial three months are crucial for new members to establish habits and feel connected to the gym community.
*   **Beyond Administration:** Onboarding should be more than just paperwork and a quick tour; it needs to focus on helping members achieve their fitness goals.
*   **Avoid Early Upselling:** Pushing personal training or supplements too soon can make new members feel like sales targets. The focus in the first 30 days should be on building competence, confidence, and good habits.
*   **Consistency is Key:** A structured onboarding process ensures every new member receives a consistent, high-quality introduction.
*   **Automation is Essential:** Relying on manual processes or memory can lead to members being overlooked. Automation, especially for communications, ensures a consistent experience for everyone.

## What a gym can do

*   **Personalized Welcome:** Greet new members warmly, introduce them to staff and long-term members, and provide a guided tour of the facility, highlighting amenities and training options.
*   **Follow-up Communication:** Send a welcome email after their in-person visit, explaining how to maximize their membership and including booking links for classes or personal training, possibly with a free trial offer.
*   **Goal Setting:** Help members define clear, measurable goals beyond general aspirations like "lose weight." Work with them to create a workout plan and consider offering a trial for relevant classes or sessions.
*   **Foster Habits and Community (Months Two & Three):**
    *   Use gym management software to track attendance and send automated reminders or encouragement if visits decline.
    *   Allow members to track their progress through a dedicated app.
    *   Invite new members to group classes to build social connections.
    *   Regularly check in with members in person and via email to address any issues.
    *   Host community events like social nights or fitness competitions.
*   **Three-Month Check-in:** Review progress, suggest workout enhancements (which can also be an upsell opportunity for classes or personal training), and invite satisfied members to a referral program.
*   **Clear Communication:** Provide all necessary initial information and explain how to make the most of their membership through a welcome email.
*   **Education and Support:** Teach members effective exercises and proper technique to empower them for long-term success.
*   **Automated Communications:** Use gym management software to send targeted messages via text and email, such as event invitations to specific interest groups.
*   **Member Tracking:** Utilize tools to monitor member engagement and identify drops in attendance early, allowing for proactive intervention to prevent cancellations.
*   **Post-First Workout Check-in:** Contact new members within 48 hours of their first solo workout to address any anxieties or concerns they might have.
*   **Structured Onboarding Checklist:** Implement a checklist for staff to ensure every member receives a consistent experience, including a gym tour, digital setup assistance, goal setting, booking their first session, and a follow-up nudge if they haven't visited within 72 hours.
*   **Leverage Technology:** Use a robust tech stack including CRM and automated communication platforms, integrated booking apps, and data-driven segmentation to personalize and scale the onboarding process.
