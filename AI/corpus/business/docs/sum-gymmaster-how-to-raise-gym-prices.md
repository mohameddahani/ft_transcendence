# How to Raise Gym Prices (GymMaster)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.gymmaster.com/blog/how-to-raise-gym-prices/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   **Inflation makes price increases necessary:** The cost of running a business typically rises by one to three percent annually due to inflation, making price adjustments essential for maintaining profitability.
*   **Avoid impulsive large price jumps:** While a significant price increase might seem appealing on paper, implementing it without careful planning can negatively impact your business.
*   **Increase perceived value first:** Before raising prices, enhance your gym's offerings. This could involve adding new classes, purchasing new equipment like tanning beds or saunas, upgrading existing machines, or introducing new merchandise or food options. The goal is to make the extra cost seem justified to members.
*   **Reward loyal members:** Offer existing members a special deal, such as locking in their current price if they pay upfront for an extended membership (e.g., six or twelve months). This makes them feel valued and encourages long-term commitment.
*   **Consider the Pareto principle:** A small percentage of your customers often generate a large portion of your sales. You could tailor offers for these high-spending members, but be mindful that members might discuss deals with each other.
*   **Research competitors, but don't rely solely on them:** Understand what other gyms with similar offerings charge, but don't let their pricing dictate yours, especially if they underprice their services. Your unique value allows you to charge more.
*   **Analyze financial scenarios:** Use reports on popular classes and membership sales to project revenue at different price points, accounting for potential member loss.
*   **Plan for the long term:** A price increase might initially lead to a decrease in members and revenue, but with the right strategy, you can rebound stronger by attracting new members willing to pay a premium. Ensure your new prices cover current costs and anticipate future increases for at least a couple of years to avoid frequent adjustments.
*   **Update all online platforms immediately:** As soon as you decide on new prices, update your website, social media, and ads to reflect them. This prevents potential new members from being surprised by a higher price.
*   **Communicate clearly and warmly:** Notify members at least a month in advance. Explain the reason for the increase, ideally framing it positively for them. Include specific monetary details, assure them it's not a regular occurrence, thank them for their loyalty, and end with a personal thank-you from leadership.
*   **Gather feedback from departing members:** Understand why members leave after a price increase. This feedback is valuable for future price adjustments and business improvements.

## What a gym can do

*   **Upgrade gym facilities and services:** Invest in new classes, equipment, or amenities like tanning beds or saunas to visibly increase the value members receive.
*   **Offer exclusive deals to existing members:** Provide options for current members to secure their existing membership rate by paying for an extended period upfront.
*   **Identify and reward top-spending members:** Use gym software to find your most valuable customers and consider offering them tailored incentives, being careful to manage potential member dissatisfaction if others don't receive similar offers.
*   **Monitor competitor pricing:** Research what other gyms in your area are charging for similar services.
*   **Utilize gym management software for analysis:** Use reporting features to analyze popular classes and membership sales, helping you model different pricing scenarios and predict revenue.
*   **Set a sustainable price increase:** Aim for a price adjustment that covers current and anticipated future costs for at least two years to avoid frequent changes.
*   **Update all public-facing pricing:** Ensure your website, social media, and advertising reflect the new prices as soon as they are decided.
*   **Provide ample notice to members:** Inform members of upcoming price changes at least one month in advance.
*   **Draft a clear and empathetic notification:** Write a letter or message that explains the price increase, its reasons, and expresses gratitude for their continued business, using a warm and human tone.
*   **Collect and analyze feedback:** Ask members who cancel their memberships why they are leaving to gain insights for future business decisions.
