# 9 Common Gym Complaints and How to Deal With Them (Glofox)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.glofox.com/blog/gym-complaints/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   Your gym's reputation is extremely valuable, attracting and keeping members, and can be damaged quickly by complaints.
*   It's crucial to handle complaints calmly and swiftly, making sure members feel heard and understood.
*   Monitor all channels where complaints might appear, including in-person, email, social media, and online reviews.
*   When a complaint arises, respond quickly and try to move the conversation to a private setting.
*   Always empathize with the customer and stay calm, even if the situation becomes heated.
*   Take ownership if your gym made a mistake, and if you disagree, try to understand the customer's perspective without being confrontational.
*   Have a clear plan to solve the problem and communicate it to the customer.
*   Maintain a system to record complaints, track their resolution, and follow up with members to ensure satisfaction.
*   Common complaints include poor hygiene, uncomfortable gym environments, rude staff, ignored issues, poor communication, broken equipment, overcrowding, last-minute cancellations, and members not seeing results.
*   Never make promises you cannot keep, as this leads to valid complaints and damages trust.

## What a gym can do

*   **Proactively manage hygiene and environment:** Implement strict cleaning protocols for all equipment and maintain a comfortable temperature and lighting in the gym.
*   **Train staff thoroughly:** Ensure all employees are well-informed, motivated, and equipped to provide excellent customer service and follow safety standards. Address any staff issues promptly.
*   **Improve communication and follow-up:** Keep track of all complaints and ensure issues are addressed and resolved. Follow up with members to confirm their satisfaction.
*   **Streamline communication channels:** Use member management software to track interactions, bookings, payments, and membership updates to prevent errors and frustration.
*   **Maintain equipment:** Promptly repair any broken or faulty equipment and ensure staff and members put equipment away after use.
*   **Manage overcrowding:** Spread out peak hours, inform members of capacity guidelines, and encourage attendance during non-peak times or use of digital fitness platforms.
*   **Handle cancellations professionally:** Give as much warning as possible for class or session cancellations and explain the reason.
*   **Support member goal achievement:** Offer additional guidance like nutrition coaching or personal training sessions to help members see results and stay motivated.
*   **Be honest with promises:** Only promise services you can deliver to build trust and avoid complaints. If a promise is missed, apologize and rectify it quickly.
