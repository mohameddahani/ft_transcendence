# 17 Gym Email Templates to Convert, Engage and Retain Members (Smart Health Clubs)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://smarthealthclubs.com/blog/gym-email-templates-to-convert-engage-retain-members/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   Email marketing is a highly effective way to communicate with both potential and current gym members, with a significant majority of consumers preferring email for brand communication.
*   Well-crafted emails can achieve various goals, such as re-engaging inactive leads, guiding new members through their initial weeks, and encouraging former members to return.
*   The article provides 17 email templates designed to cover the entire member journey, from initial interest to renewals and referrals.
*   These templates are categorized to address different stages, including lead generation, follow-ups, new member onboarding, thank you messages, reminders, feedback requests, referrals, upsells, and re-engagement for inactive or lost members.
*   The first four to six weeks of a new member's journey are particularly important for building relationships and engagement.
*   Automated emails can be set up using health club software to streamline communication for various member actions, such as class sign-ups or plan upgrades.
*   Feedback emails are valuable for understanding member satisfaction, identifying areas for improvement, and making members feel valued, with the added benefit of generating social proof.
*   Referral emails can turn existing members into advocates by offering incentives for bringing in new clients.
*   Re-engagement emails for inactive members should offer strong incentives to encourage their return, rather than just a simple "we miss you" message.

## What a gym can do

*   **Generate Leads:** Send emails offering free trials (e.g., a seven-day free trial or a free class trial) to encourage potential members to experience the gym without commitment. Offer free health and wellness consultations to attract individuals interested in a healthier lifestyle.
*   **Nurture Prospects:** Follow up with interested individuals after inquiries, scheduled tours, or trial sign-ups. Provide more details about the gym's offerings, answer questions, and remind them of any time-sensitive offers.
*   **Onboard New Members:** Send welcome emails that introduce the gym, its facilities, and staff. Offer a complimentary orientation session with a trainer and provide information about upcoming classes and events to ensure a smooth transition and early engagement.
*   **Show Appreciation:** Send thank you emails when members sign up for classes or make purchases, reinforcing their positive decision.
*   **Send Reminders:** Alert members when their membership is nearing its expiration date, offering incentives like a percentage off for renewal or an upgrade offer. Remind them about items left in their online cart, potentially with a discount.
*   **Gather Feedback:** Request feedback after a member's first class or training session using a short survey to understand their experience and identify areas for improvement.
*   **Encourage Referrals:** Implement a referral program where existing members receive an offer (e.g., a free item or service) for referring friends and family who sign up.
*   **Upsell Services:** Send personalized offers for premium memberships or additional services, highlighting the benefits and providing a discount for upgrading.
*   **Re-engage Inactive Members:** Reach out to members who haven't been active, offering strong incentives like a free personal training session or special promotions to encourage their return.
*   **Win Back Lost Members:** Send emails to former members, highlighting new events, classes, equipment, or amenities, and offering special, limited-time promotions to entice them to rejoin.
