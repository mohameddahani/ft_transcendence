# Best Practices for Renewal Email Campaigns (Mailchimp)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://mailchimp.com/resources/membership-renewal-email/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   **Renewal emails are crucial:** They help keep members, build stronger relationships, create chances to sell more, and turn current members into loyal supporters. Done poorly, they can seem pushy or get ignored.
*   **Purpose of renewal emails:** These messages remind members their membership is ending and encourage them to continue. They act as both practical notifications and strategic marketing tools, highlighting the value members receive.
*   **Benefits of renewal emails:**
    *   **Increase retention:** Many members forget to renew, not because they're unhappy. Reminders reduce this "unintentional churn," helping maintain revenue and member numbers more easily than finding new members.
    *   **Reinforce value:** Renewal is a great time to remind members why they joined by showing specific benefits they've used or goals they've achieved.
    *   **Create upgrade opportunities:** Since members are already evaluating their membership, it's a good time to suggest upgrades, extra services, or new ways to get involved.
*   **Key elements for effective emails:**
    *   **Subject line:** Needs urgency, personalization (e.g., member's name), and clarity (e.g., "Your membership expires in 7 days").
    *   **Personalization:** Use the member's name, details about their plan, benefits they've used, or statistics about their activity (e.g., "You've attended 8 webinars").
    *   **Value proposition:** Clearly state what members will lose if they don't renew, highlighting exclusive perks like discounts, priority access, or resources. Make benefits concrete (e.g., "access to 500+ templates").
    *   **Clear deadline & urgency:** Be explicit about the expiration date and what happens next. Use phrases like "Only 5 days remaining" and consider using fear of missing out (FOMO) for upcoming events.
    *   **Clear call to action (CTA):** Use action-oriented language like "Renew Now" and make the button prominent, possibly appearing multiple times in longer emails.

## What a gym can do

*   **Send a series of emails, not just one:** Start early, perhaps 45 to 60 days before expiration with a gentle reminder, then 30 days before with a stronger call to action, 7 to 10 days before with an urgent tone, and a final warning 1 to 3 days before.
*   **Make emails easy to scan:** Use short paragraphs, bullet points for benefits, bold or highlight important text (deadlines, CTAs), and ensure a mobile-friendly design with minimal distractions.
*   **Offer incentives thoughtfully:** Consider early-bird discounts (e.g., "15% off if you renew in 7 days"), bonus perks (e.g., "free webinar pass"), or loyalty rewards for long-term members. Position these as special offers, not desperate attempts.
*   **Streamline the renewal process:** Aim for fewer than three steps. Ideally, offer one-click renewal using stored payment methods. Provide multiple payment options and an auto-renewal opt-in, always making terms clear and opt-out instructions simple.
*   **Test and optimize everything:** Use A/B testing for subject lines (urgent vs. benefit-driven), send times, CTA placement, and incentive types to see what works best for your members.
*   **Follow up after renewal:** Send a thank-you email, confirm continued membership, provide onboarding for new benefits, and ask for feedback to strengthen the relationship.
*   **Start now:** Audit your current renewal emails to identify missing elements, buried CTAs, or lack of personalization, and improve them immediately.
