# Gulf States Promote Fitness and Well-Being This Ramadan (Arab Gulf States Institute)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://agsi.org/analysis/gulf-states-promote-fitness-and-well-being-this-ramadan/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   **Ramadan Focus Shift:** Traditionally, Ramadan in the Gulf States emphasized spiritual reflection, charity, family gatherings, and large meals. However, there's a new focus on fitness and healthy living, partly influenced by the coronavirus pandemic.
*   **Growing Sports Industry:** Gulf Arab states have significantly invested in sports and fitness, moving beyond just infrastructure and international events to promote local sports, national talent, and community well-being. This aligns with their goals for economic diversification and improving public health.
*   **Addressing Sedentary Lifestyles:** There's a growing trend to encourage physical activity among Gulf youth, as their lifestyles have become more sedentary compared to traditional outdoor activities like harvesting dates or pearl diving.
*   **Increased Fitness Initiatives:** This Ramadan saw a rise in local sporting events and community challenges, both virtual and in-person. Governments and organizations launched programs like "Ma’kom (With You) in Ramadan" in the UAE, virtual running competitions, cycling challenges, and daily running events in Saudi Arabia and Qatar.
*   **Health Concerns:** Obesity and diabetes are significant health issues in the Gulf region. In 2011, Kuwait had the highest obesity rate globally. A study showed 15% of Omanis have diabetes and 30% are affected by obesity.
*   **Government Interventions:** States are taking measures to address diet-related health problems. Examples include Kuwait's Salt and Fat Intake Reduction Task Force in 2013, Oman's first Obesity Conference in 2018 (where 60% of Omanis were overweight or obese), and taxes on sugary beverages in Saudi Arabia (50%) and Bahrain.
*   **Traditional Cuisine Challenges:** Traditional Gulf cuisine is often rich in fats and calories, with dishes like harees and deep-fried items commonly consumed, especially during Ramadan iftars. These meals, prepared nightly, can lead to unwanted weight gain.
*   **Healthy Eating Promotion:** Media outlets are widely sharing tips for healthy Ramadan food preparation, including advice on foods to avoid and suggestions for those with health conditions. Partnerships between weight loss companies and celebrity chefs are offering healthy recipes, and food networks are featuring shows on vegan adaptations and fish-based meals.

## What a gym can do

*   **Offer Ramadan-Specific Programs:** Design special fitness challenges, classes (like yoga or cycling), and motivational programs tailored for the fasting period, similar to government initiatives like "Ma’kom (With You) in Ramadan."
*   **Promote Virtual and In-Person Options:** Provide flexible options for members, including virtual classes and challenges, to accommodate those who prefer to exercise at home or in their neighborhoods, mirroring the virtual running competitions mentioned.
*   **Partner with Local Influencers:** Collaborate with local role models or celebrity chefs, as seen with UAE Minister Noura Al Kaabi's cycling challenge or Kcal's partnership with Chef Hala Ayash, to promote fitness and healthy eating.
*   **Educate on Healthy Eating:** Offer workshops or resources on balancing food consumption and fitness, especially during Ramadan. This could include tips on healthier meal preparation, portion control, and suitable foods for those with health conditions, aligning with the media's focus on health-conscious food advice.
*   **Highlight Health Benefits:** Emphasize how regular exercise can help address prevalent health issues like obesity and diabetes, drawing attention to the growing concerns in the region.
*   **Create Community Challenges:** Organize internal gym challenges or participate in broader community events to encourage physical activity and foster a sense of solidarity among members, similar to the "Step Together" campaign.
*   **Adapt to Cultural Practices:** Understand and respect the cultural significance of Ramadan, offering programs that allow members to maintain traditions while making healthier choices, both physically and nutritionally.
