## Key points

*   The fitness market in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) is expected to grow significantly, with an anticipated revenue increase of over eighteen percent annually between 2020 and 2025.
*   This growth is driven by a population that is becoming more health-conscious, has increasing disposable income, and is more aware of health issues like obesity, diabetes, and heart disease.
*   Some MENA countries, such as Algeria, Morocco, and Egypt, have low rates of gym participation, offering substantial opportunities for new fitness businesses.
*   The future of the gym industry is seen as a "hybrid" model, where people combine home workouts for efficiency with gym visits for social interaction.
*   Digital fitness applications have seen a boom in the MENA region, with the market projected to generate over one billion US dollars by 2025, with Turkey, Saudi Arabia, and Morocco being major contributors.

## What a gym can do

*   **Offer diverse group classes:** Focus on providing a variety of group exercise options, including Pilates, CrossFit, Zumba, aerobics, and martial arts, as this is crucial for independent gyms.
*   **Provide flexible membership options:** Introduce new membership packages that cater to customers seeking more affordable and flexible choices, such as class packages or pay-per-day options, instead of only offering longer-term memberships.
*   **Integrate wellness services:** Consider adding complementary wellness services like physiotherapy, massage, chiropractic care, performance coaching, nutrition advice, genetic testing, and body composition analysis to become a more holistic wellness provider. This trend is already seen in countries like the UAE and Saudi Arabia.
*   **Embrace the "hybrid" model:** Recognize that customers will likely combine home workouts with gym visits. Position your gym as a social hub and a place for specialized training that complements at-home fitness.
*   **Leverage technology:** Explore how your gym can integrate with or complement the growing digital fitness app market, potentially offering hybrid solutions or promoting your services through digital platforms.
*   **Cater to increasing health awareness:** Highlight how your gym's advanced equipment and programs can address lifestyle-related diseases and changing consumer health demands.
*   **Consider expansion into underpenetrated markets:** If looking to expand, countries like Algeria, Morocco, and Egypt present significant opportunities due to their low current gym penetration rates.
