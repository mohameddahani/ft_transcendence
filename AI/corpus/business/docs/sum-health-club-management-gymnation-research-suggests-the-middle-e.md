# Gymnation research suggests the Middle East is ripe for low cost gym growth (Health Club Management)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.healthclubmanagement.co.uk/health-club-management-news/Gymnation-research-suggests-the-Middle-East-is-ripe-for-low-cost-gym-growth/354904). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   **High Health Aspirations:** A significant majority of people in the UAE and KSA, specifically ninety-two percent, want to improve their health, which is a higher percentage than in the UK and US.
*   **Growing Fitness Spend:** Over forty percent of respondents have increased their spending on fitness in the past year, with more than half of these individuals citing a growing passion for fitness.
*   **Active Population:** Most people, eighty-two percent, consider themselves somewhat active, with seventy-nine percent working out at least twice a week.
*   **Health Improvement:** Sixty-four percent of respondents reported improved health over the last year, and an additional twenty-six percent maintained their health.
*   **Cost as a Major Barrier:** Half of non-gym members identify cost as the main reason they don't join a gym. Gym memberships in Dubai and Riyadh are notably expensive, being twenty-five percent pricier than in London.
*   **"Gymtimidation" is Real:** Many people are hesitant to join gyms due to unfamiliarity with equipment, a lack of exercise knowledge, and self-consciousness.
*   **Time Constraints:** Almost a quarter of respondents mentioned a lack of time, due to work or family commitments, as another barrier to exercise.
*   **New Gym-Goers:** A substantial portion of new gym members in KSA (fifty-six percent) and UAE (fifty-eight percent) have never had a gym membership before.
*   **Popular Activities:** Strength training is the most popular activity, followed by running clubs, yoga/Pilates/mindfulness classes, and Hyrox-style workouts.
*   **Low-Cost Gym Growth:** Research predicts a significant growth in low-cost gym memberships in the region, with projections of thirty to forty-five percent compound annual growth rate over the next five years.

## What a gym can do

*   **Offer Affordable Memberships:** Given that cost is a primary barrier, providing more affordable membership options is crucial to attract new members.
*   **Address "Gymtimidation":** Implement strategies to make the gym environment more welcoming and less intimidating for beginners. This could include free introductory personal training sessions and structured gym floor challenges to build community and familiarity.
*   **Provide Flexible Options:** Offer flexible membership choices to accommodate individuals with busy schedules due to work or family commitments.
*   **Increase Exercise Knowledge:** Provide resources and guidance to help members learn about equipment and exercises, which can combat self-consciousness and unfamiliarity.
*   **Focus on Popular Activities:** Emphasize and cater to popular activities like strength training, running clubs, yoga/Pilates/mindfulness, and Hyrox-style workouts in your offerings.
*   **Target New Members:** Recognize that a large percentage of potential members are new to gyms, and tailor your onboarding and support to guide these first-time users.
*   **Celebrate Progress:** The article mentions that celebrating member progress with digital messages can improve member lifetime value. Consider implementing similar recognition programs.
