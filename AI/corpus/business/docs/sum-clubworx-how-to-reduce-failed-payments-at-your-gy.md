# How to Reduce Failed Payments at Your Gym (Clubworx)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.clubworx.com/blog/how-to-reduce-failed-payments-and-protect-your-gyms-revenue). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   Failed payments are a normal part of running a gym with subscriptions, but they can lead to significant problems if not managed well.
*   The main reasons for failed payments are insufficient funds, often around payday, and card issues like expiry, loss, or fraud blocks.
*   These failures cost your gym more than just the missed payment; they also incur bank or payment gateway fees, waste staff time on manual chasing, and can damage member relationships, potentially leading to cancellations.
*   Treating payment recovery as a customer service issue rather than a debt collection problem is key to successful management.
*   Automating payment recovery processes can save many hours per week and improve member retention.

## What a gym can do

*   **Set up automated expiry reminders:** Send friendly emails or SMS messages to members before their card expires to prevent failures from happening.
*   **Establish a clear failed payment policy:** Include a straightforward clause in your membership contract or waiver and briefly discuss it during onboarding so members know what to expect if a payment bounces.
*   **Use smart retries (automated dunning):** Implement a system that automatically retries failed cards and sends follow-up communications without staff involvement. Retrying a few days after the initial failure, perhaps around a payday, can often recover funds.
*   **Offer flexible payment options and billing cycles:** Allow members to choose a billing date that aligns with their payday and provide multiple payment methods, such as direct debit alongside credit card, to reduce the chances of failure.
*   **Empower members with a self-service app:** Provide a branded member app where people can privately and instantly update their payment details from their phone, removing friction and embarrassment.
