# The fitness sector is resilient and financially strong, says HFA report (Health Club Management)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.healthclubmanagement.co.uk/health-club-management-news/The-fitness-sector-is-resilient-and-financially-strong-says-HFA-report/359130). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   The fitness industry is strong and able to recover from challenges, according to a new report from the Health and Fitness Association (HFA).
*   This report is the first detailed data survey since 2019 and gathered confidential information from a significant number of companies, representing many fitness facilities.
*   A large portion of the data came from operators in the US, with smaller contributions from Canada, EMEA, Latin America, and APAC.
*   Gyms and fitness businesses saw good financial performance in 2024, with a median revenue growth of almost ten percent.
*   The median profit margin before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization was over twenty percent, indicating healthy profitability for most operators.
*   On average, facilities experienced a net membership growth of over five percent and managed to keep two-thirds of their members.
*   The HFA president and CEO stated that the report confirms the fitness industry is a good business, highlighting its profitability and the investments operators are making to ensure continued growth.
*   The report offers detailed insights into industry performance, including revenue per location, profitability, and member retention, broken down by club size, type, and membership price.
*   The HFA's vice president of research emphasized that the report gives operators a clear view of the sector's current performance and helps them identify areas for improvement.
*   The findings also show that fitness facilities are a crucial and financially stable part of the global health economy.

## What a gym can do

*   Gym owners can use this report to compare their own performance against industry benchmarks, helping them understand where they are doing well and where there are opportunities to improve.
*   By looking at key indicators like revenue per club, profitability ratios, and member retention, gyms can gain a detailed understanding of their operational and financial health.
*   The report's breakdowns by club size, type, and membership dues level allow gyms to benchmark themselves against similar businesses.
*   The HFA offers free access to the report for its premium and standard members, as well as industry partner members, while non-members can purchase it for a fee of US$699.
*   The HFA also hosts events like its European Congress, which brings together delegates from many countries and continents, featuring prominent speakers from the fitness industry.
