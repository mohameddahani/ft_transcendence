# Creating Impactful SMS Marketing Campaigns for Gyms (WellnessLiving)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.wellnessliving.com/blog/creating-impactful-sms-marketing-campaigns-for-gyms/). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   Text messages are highly effective for gym marketing, with a very high open rate and most messages read quickly after delivery. This makes them ideal for urgent communications like filling a class or re-engaging members.
*   Successful SMS campaigns focus on good timing, personalization, and relevance, rather than just sending out mass promotions.
*   Keep messages short, ideally within 160 characters, and lead with the most important information to ensure they are read quickly.
*   Send messages during peak engagement times, such as before morning classes, at lunchtime, or before evening workouts, and avoid sending outside business hours.
*   Most consumers prefer to receive marketing texts once a week or less, with two to four promotional texts per month being a sustainable frequency for gyms. Transactional messages like reminders don't count towards this limit.
*   Each marketing text should have a single, clear call to action, and any links should go directly to the relevant page.
*   Personalize messages beyond just using a member's first name by segmenting audiences based on attendance, membership type, class preferences, and tenure.
*   Track key metrics like click-through rates, opt-out rates, and conversion rates to understand what messages resonate with your members and optimize your campaigns.
*   SMS is particularly effective for time-sensitive communications in the fitness industry, such as class reminders, open spot alerts, and renewal deadlines.
*   Younger demographics, including Gen Z, Millennials, and Gen X, are highly receptive to brand texts, but they are also sensitive to message frequency and relevance.
*   Automating SMS marketing based on member behavior (e.g., booking, attendance, milestones) is crucial for scalability and effectiveness, as it ensures the right message is sent at the right time without manual effort.

## What a gym can do

*   Implement automated class and appointment reminders to significantly increase member show-up rates and reduce no-shows. Send reminders 24 hours and again 1-2 hours before classes, and 24 hours before personal training sessions.
*   Use immediate SMS alerts for last-minute class openings or waitlist spots, including a direct link for quick booking.
*   Set up automated re-engagement messages for members who haven't checked in for a specific period, like 14 days, to encourage their return without making them feel guilty.
*   Send timely renewal reminders one to two weeks before a membership expires, highlighting member progress, upcoming events, or loyalty rewards to provide a reason to stay.
*   Utilize same-day offers for promotions like "bring a friend free" or challenge sign-ups, keeping these infrequent to avoid spamming members. Tailor these offers to different client segments.
*   Send celebratory messages for member milestones, such as their 50th visit or a one-year anniversary, to build emotional connections. Consider adding a free or discounted offer.
*   Prompt members for referrals after positive experiences or milestones, as referred members tend to have higher retention rates.
*   Choose a gym management platform that integrates SMS marketing with your booking system, membership data, and attendance records to enable behavioral triggers and automation.
*   Start SMS marketing by collecting consent during membership sign-up, then begin with transactional messages like reminders and booking confirmations before introducing promotional campaigns.
*   Leverage audience segmentation tools within your platform to send highly targeted and personalized messages that feel like individual outreach rather than mass broadcasts.
