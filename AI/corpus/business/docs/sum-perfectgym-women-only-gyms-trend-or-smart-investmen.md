# Women-Only Gyms: Trend or Smart Investment? (PerfectGym)

*A summary in our own words of [the original article](https://www.perfectgym.com/en/blog/business/women-only-gyms). Numbers in it appear in the original.*

## Key points

*   Many women prefer to exercise in an environment where they feel comfortable and not judged, without unwanted attention or feeling self-conscious.
*   A study in Spain found that a majority of gym-goers experience "gymtimidation," with women being more affected than men.
*   Reports of sexual assault in UK gyms have increased significantly since 2021, highlighting the need for safer workout spaces for women.
*   An initiative called ‘Safer Spaces to Move’ encourages gyms to implement policies against harassment, train staff on safety, and create welcoming environments for women.
*   The legality of women-only gyms varies by location, but many operate legally by being structured as private clubs or offering mixed-gender hours.
*   In some Arab countries, women-only gyms are essential due to cultural and religious values that prioritize privacy.
*   While some consider gender-exclusive gyms discriminatory, others emphasize the need for safe spaces for women. Some fitness centers address this by having women-only areas rather than entirely exclusive facilities.
*   Women-only gyms can be very profitable, as many women are willing to pay more for a comfortable and specialized fitness experience.
*   Demand for women-only gyms is growing, with online searches for them increasing by a significant percentage.
*   Some large gym chains offer dedicated women's areas, but these may not provide the same degree of specialization or unique experience as a gym exclusively for women.
*   Women's participation in fitness is increasing, particularly among middle-aged women, driven by health awareness during menopause.

## What a gym can do

*   Consider creating a women-only section within your existing gym to cater to the demand for such spaces.
*   Implement policies and staff training to address harassment and safety concerns, creating a more inclusive and secure environment for all members, especially women.
*   If considering a fully women-only gym, conduct market research to assess local demand and ensure compliance with local laws regarding gender-exclusive businesses.
*   Design a welcoming facility with equipment and classes specifically tailored to women's fitness goals.
*   Offer flexible pricing and membership plans to attract a diverse range of female members.
*   Use targeted marketing campaigns to highlight the benefits of a women-only environment, such as comfort, safety, and a supportive community.
