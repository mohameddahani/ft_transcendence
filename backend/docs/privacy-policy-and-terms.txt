This policy explains how we process information through our club management
service. The service supports club administration, staff and member accounts,
membership records, visit bookings, attendance, payment records, and feedback.
Clubs use a separate platform subscription to access management features.

In this policy, "we" means the operator identified above. A platform owner
account is an administrative role in the service; that role does not by itself
identify the legal operator. [TO CONFIRM: the privacy responsibilities of the

1.2 Information we process

Depending on your role and the features used, the service processes:

- Account information: first and last name, date of birth, gender, generated
  username, email address, telephone number, company or club name, account
  identifier, role, account status, profile image URL and image identifier,
  and account creation and update dates. Administrator records also include
  email verification status and a terms acceptance value.
- Member information: address, emergency contact telephone number, club
  administrator association, and the staff association recorded when relevant.
  Club administrators and staff can enter and update member information.
  Club administrators can enter and update staff information.
- Authentication information: password hashes; session identifiers and refresh
  token hashes; token expiry and revocation dates; verification, password
  setup, password reset, and email change token hashes; token use dates; and
  proposed new email addresses while an email change is pending.
- Session and request information: IP address, browser user-agent information,
  an inferred device description, and session creation and update dates.
  Request logs include the request method, requested URL, IP address,
  user-agent, and response status. URLs may include query information.
- Membership and subscription information: selected plans and durations,
  prices, start and expiry dates, status, club and member associations, and
  expiry notification dates. Platform subscriptions also store an amount.
- Payment records: member, club administrator and, where recorded, staff
  associations, amount, payment date, and record creation and update dates.
  The reviewed backend has no payment card or bank account fields and no
  integrated payment checkout. [TO CONFIRM: any separate payment collection
  methods and providers.]
- Visits and attendance: booked date and time, booking expiry, booking status,
  a hashed QR check-in token, related membership, attendance method, actual
  check-in time, and relevant member, club and staff identifiers.
- Feedback: text, rating, author and club associations, staff association,
  likes linked to members, status, resolution notes, resolver identifier,
  resolution date, and creation and update dates. Automated analysis can add
  a positive, neutral, or negative sentiment label and a numerical score.
- Notifications: recipient identifier, type, title, message, read status,
  and creation date.
- Wallet information: an optional Ethereum-compatible wallet address for
  administrator and member accounts, where supplied.

Some information comes directly from you. Some is entered by your club's
administrator or staff, generated during service use, or returned by the
sentiment analysis service. Emergency contacts may be people who do not hold
an account. [TO CONFIRM: how those people receive privacy information.]

1.3 Why we process information

We use this information to create and manage accounts; verify email addresses;
set, reset, and change passwords; confirm requested email changes; authenticate
sessions; and apply role, account, and club access restrictions.

We also use it to maintain club and emergency-contact records, administer
memberships and platform subscriptions, record payments, apply member and
weekly visit limits, manage opening hours and closures, book visits, and
confirm attendance manually or using QR codes.

Feedback information supports club discussions, likes, review and resolution
of feedback, sentiment analysis, and summary statistics. Membership and
subscription dates support in-service expiry reminders. Request and session
information supports service operation, troubleshooting, and authentication.
Club dashboards summarize account, membership, payment, attendance, visit,
and feedback information where those statistics are available.

Wallet information supports the reward integration described below.

1.4 Who can access information

- Platform owner accounts can view administrator account details and platform
  subscription records, manage platform plans and subscriptions, and change
  administrator account status.
- Club administrators can manage their club's staff and members and access
  club membership, payment, visit, and feedback records and statistics.
  They can confirm attendance and manage club plans and operating hours.
- Club staff can view and update members across their own club, including

  attendance, visit, and notification records, subject to access checks.

Club management operations are scoped to the associated club administrator.
Platform owner functions provide separate platform-level access. This does
not establish the access available to database or hosting personnel.
[TO CONFIRM: authorized operational personnel and their access procedures.]

Feedback is shared within the club. Eligible members can read feedback from
other members of the same club, including the author's identifier, name,
username, profile image, and role. Related administrator and staff profile
details, ratings, status, resolution notes, resolver identifiers, and dates
are also returned. Members see like counts and whether they liked a post.
Administrators and staff can also see sentiment labels and scores. Do not

1.6 Authentication cookies and tokens

The backend sets a cookie named "refresh_token" at login and replaces it when
a session is refreshed. It supports authentication. The cookie is configured
as HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, and restricted to the /api/auth path. Its Secure
flag is enabled when the backend runs in production mode. Its lifetime follows
the configured refresh token lifetime for the account role.
[TO CONFIRM: deployed cookie lifetimes for each role and production settings.]

The backend returns an access token to the client for authenticated requests.
Where the website stores or handles that token was not reviewed. Blocking or
removing the refresh cookie can prevent session refresh. The logout endpoint
clears this cookie and revokes the submitted refresh token record. Password
changes and password resets revoke existing refresh token sessions; these
actions do not prove immediate invalidation of every existing access token.

These statements describe the backend authentication cookie only. They do not
establish whether other parts of the website use analytics, advertising,
tracking, additional cookies, or browser storage.

2.5 Platform subscriptions

A club administrator's platform subscription has a plan, duration, amount,
start date, expiry date, and status. Plans include a maximum member count.
An active account, subscription, and platform plan are required for many
club operations. Subscription changes that exceed the selected plan's
member capacity are refused.

The implemented platform owner functions activate, replace, or cancel
administrator subscriptions. Cancellation changes the subscription status
immediately. Administrator subscription endpoints provide viewing functions,
without a self-service cancellation action. A replacement subscription
starts from the change time and marks the previous active subscription as
expired. Scheduled processing marks expired subscriptions accordingly.

2.6 Club memberships and visits

Club memberships are separate from the club's platform subscription.
Administrators and staff create memberships when adding members and can
assign replacement memberships. Each membership uses a plan and duration
with an expiry date. A replacement starts from the change time and marks
the previous active membership as expired. The backend has no member
self-service membership purchase or membership cancellation function.
[TO CONFIRM: club membership ordering, renewal, cancellation, and change
policies, including any treatment of unused time.]

Visit booking requires active member and club access covering the requested
visit date. Bookings must be in the future and fit the club's recorded hours
and closures. The system applies one non-cancelled visit per day and the
membership plan's weekly visit limit. Missed or expired visits can still
count toward those booking limits.

Administrators and staff confirm attendance manually or by QR code against
a visit for that day. The current check-in window begins 10 minutes before
the booked time and ends 30 minutes after it. Daily and weekly attendance
checks also apply. An eligible member can cancel their own visit while its
status is READY. This changes the booking status and keeps the record.
Cancelling a visit does not cancel a membership or platform subscription.

3.10 Memberships, subscriptions, payments, and cancellation

[TO CONFIRM: prices and currency, collection and settlement, payee, taxes,
invoices, receipts, renewals, customer cancellation channels, unused time,
refunds, credits, proration, and any applicable cancellation rights.]
Platform subscriptions are activated/replaced/cancelled through owner routes.
Cancellation sets CANCELLED immediately; administrator routes are read-only.
Member creation and membership replacement create payment rows automatically
at the plan-duration price with paidAt set to membership startDate. There is
no settlement verification, card gateway, or refund flow in this backend.
Replacement memberships and subscriptions start from the current time and
expire the previous active record; no carry-forward or proration is shown.
The schema has a membership CANCELLED status, but no membership cancellation
endpoint was found. A status enum is not a customer cancellation policy.
Visit cancellation is supported separately for the member's READY visits.
The absence of automatic renewal code does not establish off-platform
billing practices. Plan/duration deletion is blocked by historical usage;
it does not terminate accounts or delete membership or subscription history.

3.11 Operational rules, notices, and final contractual decisions

[TO CONFIRM: suspension and termination grounds, notice and appeals;
club hours and time zone handling; visit-limit and missed-visit treatment;
support commitments; content permissions; warranties; liability; remedies;
disputes; and policy or Terms change procedures.]
Visit scheduling checks club hours, special closures, daily uniqueness,
weekly limits, and access through the visit date. Attendance is confirmed
by administrators or staff, with the configured 10-minute early / 30-minute
late window. Do not convert these controls into refund or attendance-fee
policies. Server-local date calculations are used in several checks;
production time zone and user presentation were not verified.
Hourly jobs create database expiry notifications within the next 24 hours;
they do not send those reminders by email or guarantee delivery. Other
notification types in the enum do not prove a live messaging workflow.
No service-level guarantee, reward guarantee, refund rule, or automatic
account erasure is established by this review. Obtain business and legal
review of all remaining placeholders before publishing either document.
